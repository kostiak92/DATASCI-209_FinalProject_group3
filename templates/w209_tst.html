Tourism and Restaurants Map – Explore Local Favorites & Attractions

Welcome to the Interactive Tourism and Restaurants Map created using Yelp and Kaggle data! This map is designed to help food enthusiasts, travelers, and curious explorers find highly rated restaurants and nearby tourist attractions across the United States.

Some Features include:

  1. 🔍 Restaurant Search: Look up specific restaurants by name.
  2. ⭐ Yelp Ratings Filter: Use the sidebar to explore restaurants by Yelp star ratings (1–5 stars) and number of reviews (100–5000+).
  3. 📍 Tourist Attractions Filter: View major landmarks and filter for popular tourist destinations like Central Park, Alcatraz Island, and Disneyland Park.

Explore the map and discover different types of popular restaurants near tourist attractions to plan your next food-centric travel adventures.

Note: Major cities like LA, NYC, Chicago, and SF are missing from the Yelp Open Dataset due to privacy and licensing limits, so some popular restaurants may not appear on the map.

How to Use the Graphs

Left Graph: Top Restaurants by Restaurant Type or State

  • Each colored dot represents a restaurant, categorized by type/state.
  • Hover over a point to view rating, review count, and type.
  • Ctrl + Click on a point to open its location in Google Maps.

Middle Graph: Filtering by Brush Select

  • Click and drag to draw a selection box ("brush").
  • This filters restaurants based on User Rating and Review Count.
  • Use this to focus on high-rated or frequently reviewed restaurants.
  • Ctrl + Click on a point to open its location in Google Maps.

Right Graph: Brush Display

  • Displays only the data points selected from the middle graph.
  • Use this view to analyze the filtered selection in detail.
  • Ctrl + Click on a point to open its location in Google Maps.
